## Research

[Adapting to Artificial Intelligence: Worker Skill Investment after ChatGPT](https://github.com/rajibor24/ai-exposure-skill-investment) (job market paper, draft in progress) If a technology arrives that can do part of your job, and no institution is retraining you, do you retrain yourself? Workers in more exposed occupations became 43 percent more likely to earn an AI certification, but they did not train more overall: certification in data science and analytics fell 23 percent, and in cloud, software and IT 16 percent, in the same workers over the same window, while safety and compliance credentials did not move and total certification did not rise. What changed was the composition of training, not its level. Employer demand moved in the same direction, with the share of AI-skill vacancies in exposed occupations rising 49 percent, but workers began certifying around 2023Q2 while the vacancy increase only became clearly detectable around 2023Q4. A placebo at the earlier release of GPT-3, the larger capability jump that almost no ordinary worker encountered, yields a precise null: the response was to the technology becoming visible, not to it becoming capable. Panel of 1.5 million U.S. workers, quarterly, 2018 to 2025.

[Predicting Corporate Default: A Dynamic Logit Benchmark versus Machine Learning](https://github.com/rajibor24/credit-risk-default-prediction) A dynamic logit hazard model against XGBoost on 157,556 firm-years of U.S. public firms (Compustat and CRSP, 1980 to 2022). XGBoost ranks slightly better throughout, but the sharper difference is in calibration: both models track realized default rates closely in ordinary years and fail in opposite directions during macro shocks. Joint work with [Kunal Garg](https://github.com/gargk98).

[Effect of Fast Internet Access on Human Capital Formation: Evidence from India](https://github.com/rajibor24/bharatnet-human-capital) Instrumental-variable estimates of the BharatNet broadband rollout on school enrollment and field-of-study choice in rural India.
