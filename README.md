## Research

**[Retraining Without More Training: AI Exposure and the Composition of Skill Investment](https://github.com/rajibor24/ai-exposure-skill-investment)**
(job market paper, draft in progress)
If a technology arrives that can do part of your job, and no institution is retraining you,
do you retrain yourself? Workers in more exposed occupations became 39 percent more likely
to earn an AI certification, but they did not train more overall: certification in data
science fell 23 percent and in cloud and IT 15 percent, in the same workers over the same
window, while an unrelated category did not move. What changed was the composition of
training, not its level. A placebo at the earlier release of GPT-3, the larger capability
jump that almost no ordinary worker encountered, yields a precise null: the response was to
the technology becoming visible, not to it becoming capable. Panel of 1.5 million U.S.
workers, quarterly, 2018 to 2025.

**[Predicting Corporate Default: A Dynamic Logit Benchmark versus Machine Learning](https://github.com/rajibor24/credit-risk-default-prediction)**
A dynamic logit hazard model against XGBoost on 157,556 firm-years of U.S. public firms
(Compustat and CRSP, 1980 to 2022). XGBoost ranks slightly better throughout, but the
sharper difference is in calibration: both models track realized default rates closely in
ordinary years and fail in opposite directions during macro shocks. Joint work with
[Kunal Garg](https://github.com/gargk98).

**[Effect of Fast Internet Access on Human Capital Formation: Evidence from India](https://github.com/rajibor24/bharatnet-human-capital)**
Instrumental-variable estimates of the BharatNet broadband rollout on school enrollment
and field-of-study choice in rural India.
