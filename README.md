## Research

**AI Exposure and Career Trajectories** (job market paper, in progress)
Do workers move before automation reaches them? I use the November 2022 release of
ChatGPT as a shock to perceived AI capability, and track whether workers in more exposed
occupations went on to switch employers, change occupations, or pick up new credentials.
Exposure comes from the AIOE index, fixed at each worker's occupation before the shock.
The panel covers 1.5 million U.S. workers, built from resume records on 1.6 billion job
spells.

**[Predicting Corporate Default: A Dynamic Logit Benchmark versus Machine Learning](https://github.com/rajibor24/credit-risk-default-prediction)**
A dynamic logit hazard model against XGBoost on 157,556 firm-years of U.S. public firms
(Compustat and CRSP, 1980 to 2022). XGBoost ranks slightly better throughout, but the
sharper difference is in calibration: both models track realized default rates closely in
ordinary years and fail in opposite directions during macro shocks. Joint work with
[Kunal Garg](https://github.com/gargk98).

**[Effect of Fast Internet Access on Human Capital Formation: Evidence from India](https://github.com/rajibor24/bharatnet-human-capital)**
Instrumental-variable estimates of the BharatNet broadband rollout on school enrollment
and field-of-study choice in rural India.
